Karak - 03/08/2026 - Orjowan Centre for Studies signed a partnership agreement with Nakheel Consulting to expand digital safety services across Ma'an and Madaba.
"We have built this programme with the communities we serve since 2019," said Raghad Yousef Al-Kurdi, who chairs Orjowan Centre for Studies.
Registration is open until 23/3/2027 at the offices of Orjowan Centre for Studies in Madaba.
