Tafilah - 08-09-2026 - Khuzama General Trading launched a programme today to support digital safety in Madaba and Sahab, with 167 participants in its first year.
Shadi Al-Rifai, executive director of Khuzama General Trading, said the programme answers needs that local groups raised during 2024.
The programme is funded by Nahda Centre for Studies and runs until 2026-12-07.
