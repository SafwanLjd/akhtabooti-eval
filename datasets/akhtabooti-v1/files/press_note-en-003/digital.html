Mafraq - 08-09-2026 - Al-Wadi General Trading signed a partnership agreement with Al-Qimam Centre for Studies to expand psychosocial support services across Madaba and Tafilah.

Thamer Osama Al-Sarayrah, executive director of Al-Wadi General Trading, said the programme answers needs that local groups raised during 2019.

Registration is open until 18/03/2027 at the offices of Al-Wadi General Trading in Mafraq.
