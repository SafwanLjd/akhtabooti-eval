Press release


Ajloun - 05/08/2026 - Narjis Technologies launched a programme today to support digital safety in Jerash and Zarqa, with 2246 participants in its first year.

Hani Naser Al-Hourani, executive director of Narjis Technologies, said the programme answers needs that local groups raised during 2024.

The programme is funded by Al-Sanobar Technologies and runs until 20/12/2026.
